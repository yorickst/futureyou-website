Helpful Details

When you contact support, please include your app version, device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful when they do not include private information.

Notifications

If reminders are not arriving, check that notifications are enabled for Future Yourself in your device settings and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not blocking alerts.
